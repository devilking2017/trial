Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Gau Amrit Dairy located?

Gau Amrit Dairy is listed at Aliganj, Etah, Uttar Pradesh – 207247, India. Contact us to confirm delivery coverage for your address.

How can I place an order?

Add products to your cart, enter your details and select your payment preference. Then click “Send Order on WhatsApp” and press Send in WhatsApp.

Is the online payment option active?

No. Prepaid is currently a preference only. A real payment gateway must be integrated before the website can accept online payments.

Can I confirm availability before ordering?

Yes. Contact the dairy by phone or WhatsApp to confirm product availability, quantities and delivery timing.

Can I request a different quantity?

Use the plus and minus buttons in the cart to change quantities. Product units and minimum quantities are shown with each product.
